PCCST503 Assignment 1 demonstration scenarios

The executable src/main.cpp constructs these scenarios directly.

1. Basic Reachability
   S -> A -> B -> G
   Expected: unique valid route.

2. Bad State Avoidance
   Direct route contains a bad state.
   Expected: safe detour is selected.

3. Safety Margin
   Two safe routes have different proximity to a bad state.
   Expected: safety penalty influences the selected route.

4. Dynamic Transition
   A previously useful transition becomes unavailable.
   Expected: D* Lite repairs the route.

5. Goal Update
   The goal changes after an initial plan.
   Expected: planner replans toward the new goal.

6. Transition Addition
   A shortcut is inserted.
   Expected: planner can use the new transition.
